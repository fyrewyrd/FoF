# Monetization

### Guiding Rules
- No direct stat or power purchases.
- Cosmetics and expression are the primary honest revenue.
- Any progression acceleration must remain optional and bounded.
- Failure systems must have visible pity/protection so they do not feel predatory.

### Planned / Discussed Avenues

**1. Monthly Cosmetic Pack (~$5)**
- Dyes, hairs, accessories, 1–2 skins.
- Tradable / sellable contents preferred.
- Low pressure, high honesty.

**2. Campaign / Quest Pass (non-expiring)**
- Bought once or earned.
- Long calendar-style track.
- Curated rewards that help prepare players for higher Tower floors (materials, protectors, QoL, cosmetics).

**3. Tower Permits**
- Limited entries into the Tower.
- Can be earned and also purchased.
- Primary access gate that protects content production speed.

**4. Convenience & Quality-of-Life**
- Storage, inventory help, visual filters, etc.
- Must never become required for basic play.

**5. Strain Relief Items**
- Limited-use items with cooldown that remove Strain stacks.
- Small additional sink.

### Future Considerations
- Gifting systems (whales buying cosmetics/convenience for others).
- Seasonal cosmetics.
- Housing / profile expression once those systems exist.

### Design Stance
Whales are not ignored, but the systems should not require whale spending to feel complete. Frugal and time-rich players should still have dignity and economic roles (gathering, crafting, knowledge).

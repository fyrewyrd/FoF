
**Forced Spawns**

-Artifact Seals-
Trade an item to an ancient temple and get a boss to appear

-


**Timed Spawns**
-World Bosses-
These spawn at random times days after defeat


**Conditional Spawns**

-World Bosses-
If a certain amount of dungeons have been defeated then these may appear

-Rift Breaks-
When a dungeon hasn't been defeated in a certain amount of time, the mobs will spill out and attack the nearest town

# 01 — Core Pillars

These are the non-negotiable design values. Every system should be checked against them.

### 1. Meaningful Investment
Players should feel attached to specific weapons, armor pieces, and characters. Deep vertical investment is preferred over constant horizontal chasing of new base items.

### 2. Failure with Agency
Failure (especially on upgrades) creates tension and sinks, but should rarely feel purely spiteful. Pity systems, visible progress, and the ability to keep the base item on failure are important.

### 3. Knowledge Has Value
Secrets, books, puzzles, and overworld discoveries should meaningfully help inside the Tower and other high-end content. Information can be traded or shared.

### 4. Shared Danger, Soft Pressure
The Tower remains a mostly shared space. Strain / decay creates personal urgency without fully isolating players. Cooperation, carrying, and conflict are all possible.

### 5. Honest Monetization
No direct power purchases. Cosmetics, convenience, and limited access (permits) are acceptable. Progression acceleration must stay optional and bounded.

### 6. Catch-Up Without Invalidation
New Tower floors should extend the relevance of existing gear. Illusion/cosmetic systems let players keep visual identity. Content does not permanently leave players behind.

### 7. Multiple Valid Playstyles
Crafters, gatherers, explorers, support players, and combat specialists should all have dignified roles and ways to contribute to the economy and social fabric.

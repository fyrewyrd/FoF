Pets and Summons carry their own types of damage

Pets have their own stats and are often buffed by Pet buffs
Summons will often borrow from the stats of the Summoner and each Summon uses different stats

In the case of the Necromancer the tank will gain hp/def based off of the Summoner's HP
Their attacking units will borrow from the int stat
The Summoner class's elements will take from the stats that they are closely related to so 
the Golem avatar will borrow from the Fortitude stat and Djinn will borrow from the Attack stat

Pets will have different physical stats like slash, impact, and pierce. Bleed attacks that carry stat debuffs like Lacerated which carries slash down and stacks would help them do more damage on top of the fact that they do more damage when a bleed effect is present so other bleed types will also aid them as well as Pet buffs. A Tiger will benefit from Lacerate where as a Falcon from X and Bear from Crush

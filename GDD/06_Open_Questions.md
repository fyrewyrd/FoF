# Open Questions

Living list of unresolved design decisions. Move items out of this note as they are decided and documented elsewhere.

### Progression
- Final level cap for weapon upgrades (currently exploring +25).
- Exact pity numbers and whether hard pity is guaranteed success or a large bonus.
- How aggressive rank-up material/weapon consumption should be.
- Gem socket rules and extraction.

### Tower
- Final Strain stacking rate and penalty strength after playtesting.
- Exact permit counts per day/week.
- How many secret sub-dungeons and conditional elites exist at MVP.
- Networking approach (additive scenes vs high-altitude same-scene instances).

### Economy
- Precise material drop rates from Abyss / Spire / Tower.
- Whether any materials should be account-bound or fully tradable.
- Daily supply box contents and limits.

### Monetization
- Final price and contents of the monthly cosmetic pack.
- Whether the Campaign Pass is purely paid, earnable, or hybrid.
- Scope of convenience items at launch.

### World & Narrative
- Magic system depth (still under-explored).
- Nation structure and major story threads.
- How strongly lore is delivered through environmental storytelling vs dialogue vs items.

### Technical
- Preferred Mirror interest management + scene strategy for dungeons.
- How much of the old uMMORPG instance-in-the-sky pattern to keep during early prototyping.

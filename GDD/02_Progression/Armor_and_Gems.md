# Armor & Gems

### Armor Progression
- Armor parts can be found, crafted, and upgraded.
- **The Spire** is a primary source of special armor pieces tied to celestial / gladiator lore.
- Armor can receive **gem slots / ability sockets** as a mid-to-late progression goal.

### Gem / Ability System
- Gems or abilities are socketed into armor.
- This provides a parallel investment track to weapons.
- Exact socket rules, gem tiers, and extraction rules are still open for tuning.

### Visual Identity
When new gear appears on higher Tower floors, players can use **illusion / cosmetic items** so their preferred appearance is preserved while using stronger stats.

### Open Design Notes
- How many gem slots open at what progression points?
- Can gems be extracted and reused?
- How strongly should set bonuses from Spire armor pull players toward specific builds?

# Materials Economy

### Material Tiers (Working Names)
- **Common Scrap** — early upgrades
- **Tempered Ore** — mid upgrades
- **Refined Alloy** — high upgrades
- **Master’s Essence** — prestige (+21 to +25)

### Cost per Attempt (Rank 1 baseline)

| Upgrade   | Common | Tempered | Refined | Master’s |
|-----------|--------|----------|---------|----------|
| +1–5      | 8–12   | —        | —       | —        |
| +6–10     | 5      | 6–10     | —       | —        |
| +11–15    | —      | 8–12     | 4–7     | —        |
| +16–20    | —      | 4        | 10–15   | 2–4      |
| +21–25    | —      | —        | 8       | 8–14     |

Higher ranks reduce expected material cost.

### Sources
- **The Abyss** — primary weapon materials
- **The Tower** — Master’s Essence and rare protectors
- **The Spire** — armor-related materials
- **Overworld** — base materials, recipes, knowledge
- Daily / Campaign Pass supplies — convenience materials and permits

### Design Goals
- Gatherers and crafters remain useful.
- Materials are a real bottleneck at high levels without feeling impossible for dedicated players.
- Cash convenience stays optional.

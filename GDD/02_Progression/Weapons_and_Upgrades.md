# Weapons & Upgrades

### Ranks
Weapons have **Ranks 1–5**.
- Rank determines the maximum upgrade level and provides a success rate bonus.
- Ranking up consumes another weapon of the same (or lower) type.

| Rank | Max Upgrade | Base Success Bonus |
|------|-------------|--------------------|
| 1    | +5          | 0%                 |
| 2    | +10         | +5%                |
| 3    | +15         | +8%                |
| 4    | +20         | +12%               |
| 5    | +25         | +15%               |

### Upgrade Levels (+1 to +25)
Power primarily comes from leveling a weapon through repeated upgrade attempts that can fail.

**Base success rates (Rank 1):**

| Range     | Base Success |
|-----------|--------------|
| +1 to +5  | 80–90%       |
| +6 to +10 | 60–75%       |
| +11 to +15| 40–55%       |
| +16 to +20| 25–40%       |
| +21 to +25| 12–25%       |

Higher ranks add their bonus (capped near 95%).

### Pity System
- Each failure on a specific weapon adds **+3%** success chance for the next attempt on that weapon.
- Soft pity after ~4 consecutive failures.
- Hard pity / guarantee around 8–10 failures on the same level.
- Blacksmith dialogue can flavor the pity ("the hammer is learning the metal").

**Important:** On failure the upgrade materials are consumed, but the **base weapon is kept**. Full destruction of the item is avoided to reduce rage-quit potential.

### Crafting
Weapons can be:
- Crafted via dialogue-driven processes with some RNG
- Found in the world / activities
- Used as feeders for rank-ups

Benches / NPC blacksmiths can level up and improve success rates or reduce material loss.

### Design Intent
Players who commit to one main weapon feel strong ownership. Collectors and synergy players have room to experiment. The failure curve creates material demand without pure pay-to-win.

# 00 — Overview

**Working Title:** Fate of Faoria (Project F)

### Elevator Pitch
A small-scale online world focused on deep personal investment, meaningful failure, shared dangerous spaces, and community-driven progression — built as a counter-example to modern extraction-focused live-service design.

### Core Fantasy
Players grow attached to individual weapons and characters through time, risk, and craft. The Tower is a strained, dangerous dream-space that tests preparation and knowledge. The overworld teaches secrets that make the Tower survivable. Strong players can help weaker ones, and knowledge itself has value.

### Design Philosophy
- Cosmetics and expression as primary honest revenue
- Failure creates tension and material demand without pure spite
- One primary content gate (The Tower) to protect production speed
- Shared spaces with soft personal pressure (Strain) rather than pure hard instancing
- New floors extend the life of existing gear instead of forcing constant replacement
- Players who explore and craft have real roles alongside combat specialists

### Current Scope
MVP focused on proving the core loops:
- Weapon investment & ranking
- Tower with Strain system
- Abyss / Spire as supporting activities
- Basic material economy
- Social carry / knowledge sharing

# The Abyss

### Structure
Players fight through **4 towers**, then face the **Demon King** who has rotating generals.

### Primary Role
Main source of **weapons and weapon materials** (especially Tempered Ore → Master’s Essence range).

### Design Notes
- Rotating generals keep the final encounter fresh.
- Weapon drops should not flood the market with ready-to-use high-rank items; the crafting and upgrade investment must remain meaningful.
- Serves as a more repeatable, less knowledge-gated activity than the main Tower.

### Open Questions
- Exact number of weekly/daily entries or any soft gates.
- How strongly the rotating generals change required strategies.
- Relationship between Abyss clear speed and material income targets.

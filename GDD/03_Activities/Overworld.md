# Overworld

### Role
The living world that teaches, supplies, and connects everything else.

### Key Functions
- Drops accessories and base crafting materials.
- Contains **secrets, books, puzzles, and NPCs** that grant knowledge useful inside the Tower and other high-end activities.
- Hosts special crafting locations and nodes.
- Supports daily quests, weekend activities, and social gathering.

### Design Intent
Exploration and curiosity should be rewarded with information and soft advantages, not just raw power. Knowledge becomes a tradable and shareable resource.

Players who prefer gathering, crafting, or discovery still contribute meaningfully to the wider player economy and to Tower success.

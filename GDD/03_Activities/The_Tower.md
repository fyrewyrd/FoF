# The Tower

High-end shared activity. Dangerous, knowledge-sensitive, and the primary prestige space.

### Fantasy & Lore
The Tower is a strained dream-scenario brought to life by Faoria’s power. Entering it puts stress on the world. Players gain strength here to face threats sent into the world. Because the power is limited, presence itself creates Strain.

### Core Rules
- Mostly **shared** space (not fully instanced).
- **Strain system** creates personal time pressure.
- Secret sub-dungeons, conditional elite spawns, and boss arenas can be temporary/private.
- Traps and environmental danger help control camping and punish unprepared players.
- Limited **permits** control overall entry volume.

### Strain System (Current Numbers)
- Grace period: first 2 minutes
- +1 Strain stack every 8 minutes
- Each stack: –4% outgoing damage/healing/movespeed, +4% damage taken

| Time in Tower | Stacks | Penalty     |
|---------------|--------|-------------|
| 0–2 min       | 0      | None        |
| 2–10 min      | 1      | –4% / +4%   |
| 10–18 min     | 2      | –8% / +8%   |
| 18–26 min     | 3      | –12% / +12% |
| 26–34 min     | 4      | –16% / +16% |
| 34–42 min     | 5      | –20% / +20% |
| 42+ min       | 6+     | Rising      |

Boss/secret instances pause Strain. Leaving the Tower clears it.

A 1-hour cooldown item can remove a few stacks.

### Supporting Systems
- **Rotating hot zones** for material competition and anti-camping.
- Overworld secrets that reveal safer routes, trap warnings, or bonus spawns.
- Special crafting nodes inside the Tower that create carry/trade opportunities.

### Design Intent
Create urgency and prestige while preserving the ability for players to help each other, compete, or simply exist in the same dangerous space.

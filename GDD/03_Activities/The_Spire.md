# The Spire

### Fantasy
Players fight **celestials** for special armor parts based on gladiatorial / celestial lore.

### Primary Role
Armor progression path and source of unique armor pieces that can carry set bonuses or strong passives.

### Design Notes
- Provides a parallel track to weapon investment so players are not only chasing swords.
- Lore ties give the pieces identity and collection value.
- Can feed into gem/socket systems later.

### Open Questions
- How many distinct celestial armor sets exist at MVP.
- Whether Spire has its own entry limits or shares pressure systems with the Tower.
- Strength of set bonuses vs generic upgraded armor.

Active Abilities
These abilities can go into each slot and in some cases upgrade
Skills - these can be buffs or attacks and will all depend on certain active buffs
Ultimate - these are often on a timer and may perform differently depending on the unit
Defensive - these are timer dependant

Active Buffs
This name may change. taps and hold can grant a stub buffs in single or stacked form for other abilities to absorb to activate. some may absorb 1 or more or remove a debuff or stacked debuffs from a target to activate them as well (dagger ability 5 may destroy 5 poison stacks for increased dmg or may require a certain active buff in order to use an attack etc)

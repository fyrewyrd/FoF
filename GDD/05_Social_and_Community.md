# Social & Community

### Desired Environment
A place where players can help each other, share knowledge, trade, and form groups without the game constantly pushing pure competition or extraction.

### Supported Behaviors
- **Carrying** — stronger players helping weaker ones (inspired by communities like Warframe).
- **Knowledge trading** — secrets and Tower information have value.
- **Crafting services** — special nodes and benches create opportunities for players to trade combat help for crafting access.
- **Conflict** — shared dangerous spaces will attract some antagonistic play; the design accepts this rather than trying to eliminate it completely.

### Systems That Encourage Healthy Social Play
- Strain and permits create natural group formation windows.
- Hot zones create temporary competition without permanent locking of content.
- Illusion/cosmetics reduce the social pressure of "looking undergeared."
- Non-expiring Campaign Pass and catch-up friendly floor design reduce permanent left-behind feelings.

### Open Design Areas
- Exact reward structures for successfully helping lower-progress players.
- Tools for forming groups and communicating knowledge.
- How strongly the game should surface or reward mentoring behavior.
